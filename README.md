# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales and Customer Behavior Analysis

**Project Year:** 2025  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel | Power Query | Microsoft Power BI | DAX

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes one year of e-commerce sales data to evaluate sales performance, product category contribution, customer purchasing behavior, pricing patterns, profitability, seasonal trends, and customer satisfaction.

The project uses Microsoft Excel, Power Query, and Power BI to clean and transform data, develop interactive dashboards, track key performance indicators (KPIs), and generate actionable business insights.

**Total Sales Analyzed:** ₹2,97,125.56

The primary objective is to support data-driven business decisions, optimize product pricing, improve customer experience, and identify opportunities for sustainable revenue growth.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, quantity sold, and order performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Evaluate monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Understand the relationship between product pricing and sales performance.
- Analyze customer review scores and review counts.
- Compare regional sales performance to identify growth opportunities.
- Evaluate sales and profit performance against business targets.
- Develop practical recommendations for pricing, promotions, and customer retention.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The project uses the following datasets:

- **List of Orders.csv** – Contains order dates, customer names, states, and cities.
- **Order Details.csv** – Contains order IDs, product details, sales amounts, quantities, and profit.
- **Sales target.csv** – Contains monthly sales targets by product category.

The datasets were prepared and transformed using Power Query before being used for analysis and dashboard development.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning and Transformation

The following data preparation activities were performed:

- Inspected the datasets and reviewed column names and data types.
- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
- Standardized date, numeric, and text fields.
- Cleaned and standardized category and location information where required.
- Checked order IDs and common fields before merging tables.
- Combined relevant datasets using appropriate keys.
- Reviewed sales, quantity, profit, and target fields for consistency.
- Validated the transformed data before creating visualizations.
- Prepared analysis-ready tables for reporting and dashboard development.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard supports analysis of key business metrics, including:

- **Total Sales:** ₹2,97,125.56
- **Total Profit:** Measures the profit generated during the reporting period.
- **Total Quantity Sold:** Tracks the number of units sold.
- **Total Orders:** Measures order volume.
- **Average Order Value:** Evaluates average sales value per order.
- **Monthly Sales:** Tracks changes in sales over time.
- **Category-wise Sales:** Compares the contribution of product categories.
- **Sales Target Achievement:** Compares actual sales with available targets.

## ⭐ Customer Review Analysis

The following review metrics were identified in the project analysis:

- **Total Review Score:** 527K
- **Average Review Count:** 3.03
- **High Review Score:** Above 4
- **Low Review Score:** Below 2.5

Products with strong customer ratings may be suitable for advertising, cross-selling, and promotional campaigns. Products with lower ratings should be reviewed to identify possible quality, delivery, or customer experience issues.

*Review scores and review counts represent the fields available in the dataset; they should not automatically be interpreted as the number of unique customers or verified purchases.*

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Product Quality and Customer Experience

- Improve product descriptions, images, and packaging quality.
- Investigate customer complaints and address recurring product issues.
- Improve delivery reliability and reduce avoidable delivery costs.
- Consider warranties for eligible electronic products.
- Use customer feedback to guide product improvements.

### 2. Optimize Pricing and Promotions

- Evaluate products in the ₹100–₹300 price range identified in the analysis.
- Introduce affordable product bundles and combo offers.
- Test limited-time discounts, weekend offers, and seasonal promotions.
- Offer gift vouchers and loyalty rewards where commercially appropriate.
- Evaluate profit margins before applying discounts.

### 3. Strengthen Product and Category Marketing

- Increase promotion of high-performing products and categories.
- Use product ratings as one input when selecting products for campaigns.
- Explore cross-selling and complementary product bundles.
- Review underperforming products before increasing advertising expenditure.
- Track campaign results to assess whether promotions improve profitable sales.

### 4. Plan for Seasonal Demand

- Use historical monthly sales to support campaign planning.
- Schedule promotions around periods of stronger sales.
- Prepare inventory and delivery capacity for anticipated demand.
- Compare sales patterns across product categories and regions.
- Evaluate promotional performance after each campaign.

### 5. Improve Profitability

- Monitor profit alongside revenue and sales volume.
- Investigate products with high sales but low profit.
- Review procurement, packaging, and logistics costs.
- Compare actual sales with monthly category targets.
- Prioritize strategies that improve sustainable profit rather than revenue alone.

## ⚠️ Data and Analysis Considerations

- Findings are based on the available e-commerce dataset and the period covered.
- Missing values, duplicates, and data types were reviewed during data preparation.
- Outliers were identified but not automatically removed because they may represent genuine high-value orders or unusual sales activity.
- Sales performance can vary across product categories, regions, customers, and time periods.
- Customer review scores indicate observed feedback and do not necessarily establish the cause of sales performance.
- Correlation and observed trends do not establish causation.
- Business recommendations should be validated against updated data, operating costs, and profit margins before implementation.
